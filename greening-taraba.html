A youth-led climate justice initiative

Greening Taraba mobilizes young people, faith communities, schools, women and local stakeholders to:

Plant and nurture trees
Promote environmental stewardship
Raise climate change awareness
Restore degraded landscapes
Strengthen community resilience
Build a greener and more peaceful Taraba
Our vision

A climate-resilient Taraba where people and nature thrive together.

Our mission

To inspire collective action for environmental sustainability through tree planting, climate education and community engagement.
