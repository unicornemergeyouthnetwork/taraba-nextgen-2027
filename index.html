Who we are

Young people. Shared purpose.
Lasting change.

A youth-led network of volunteers and community organisers working across Taraba State, Nigeria. While we continue to grow as a grassroots youth network, our work focuses on peacebuilding, youth and young women’s empowerment and leadership, gender equity, climate justice, interfaith collaboration, and community development.

  1. 01

    Our Vision

    To build a just, inclusive, and sustainable society driven by empowered youth.

  2. 02

    Our Mission

    To equip and mobilise youths through education, advocacy, and grassroots organising for sustainable social transformation.

Before you apply

Is NEXTGEN
for you?

Review the five requirements before starting your application.

Start your application ↗
  1. 01

    Age

    Applicants must be between 18 and 35 years old at the time of the conference.

  2. 02

    Simple Data & Survey Ability

    Applicants should be able to participate in simple surveys, questionnaires, data collection or basic community information-gathering activities when required.

  3. 03

    Local Delegate Residency

    Applicants seeking representation as a local delegate from a Taraba LGA must currently reside in the LGA they are applying to represent, regardless of their State or LGA of origin.

  4. 04

    Interest & Community Engagement

    Applicants should demonstrate interest in agriculture, enterprise, climate action, youth development, innovation or community development.

  5. 05

    Full Participation

    Applicants must be willing and available to participate fully from 24–26 June 2027 and complete reasonable pre- or post-conference activities required of selected delegates.

Please note: Meeting the eligibility requirements does not automatically guarantee selection. Applications will be reviewed based on the conference selection criteria and available delegate spaces.

Application Terms, Privacy & Media Consent

This notice explains the terms governing applications to the Taraba NEXTGEN Youth Stakeholders Conference 2027 and how Unicorn Youth Development Network handles information submitted by applicants.

1. Application Terms

By submitting an application, you confirm that the information you provide is accurate and has been provided by you. Submission does not guarantee selection for the conference. Applications may be reviewed based on eligibility requirements, conference objectives, available spaces and the selection process.

False, misleading or deliberately inaccurate information may result in an application being disqualified.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you voluntarily provide through the application form, including your contact details, demographic information, background, interests, experience, motivation and responses to conference-related questions.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Applicant information may be used to review applications, select and communicate with participants, plan and administer the conference, provide relevant conference updates and coordinate appropriate post-conference activities.

4. Access and Handling

Applicant information will be accessible only to authorised members of Unicorn Youth Development Network and persons involved in the application, selection or conference administration where access is reasonably necessary.

We do not sell applicants' personal information or use it for unrelated commercial purposes.

5. Applicant Consent

By submitting the application, you acknowledge this notice and consent to the collection and use of the information you provide for the purposes described above.

6. Photographs, Videos & Media Consent

Photographs, video recordings and other audiovisual materials may be captured during the Taraba NEXTGEN Youth Stakeholders Conference 2027.

By providing media consent, you permit Unicorn Youth Development Network (UYDN) to use photographs, videos and recordings in which you may appear for legitimate conference and organisational purposes, including event documentation, reports, publicity, social media, websites, publications and promotion of current or future UYDN programmes and activities.

UYDN will use such materials responsibly and in connection with its programmes, activities and communications.

7. Service providers and your choices

The application form sends your answers to a Google Apps Script service used by UYDN. Google processes this transmission as a service provider. External WhatsApp and social-media links are optional and are subject to those services' privacy practices.

You may contact UYDN to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to withdraw consent. Requests are subject to applicable legal requirements. Withdrawing application-processing consent may prevent UYDN from continuing to assess your application. Media consent is separate and optional.

You may also raise a data-protection complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Notice version: 19 October 2026.

8. Contact & Privacy Enquiries

If you have questions about your application or how your information is handled, contact Unicorn Youth Development Network:

unicornyouthdevelopmentnetwork@gmail.com
