About the Convener

John Geraji Jacob convened Taraba NEXTGEN Conference 2026 through Unicorn Youth Development Network. John Geraji Jacob is a Chartered Banker (ACIB) and Associate International Retail Banker (AIRB), currently serving as a Relationship Manager at Access Bank PLC, Nigeria. He is the Convener of Greening Taraba, where he leads youth-driven initiatives focused on leadership, climate action, and community development. John is the founder of Unicorn Youth Development Network and coordinates the Taraba NextGen Conference, 2026, A major youth-led stakeholders Conference to be held in Jalingo. He has collaborated with organisations such as Nigerian Women Lead and OMNIA Interfaith Peacemaker Team on community organising, gender advocacy, and peacebuilding. Passionate about youth empowerment, social justice, and sustainable development, John works at the intersection of finance, advocacy, and grassroots mobilisation to drive transformative change in North-Eastern Nigeria.
